About Yoru no Toshokan

夜の図書館, "the night library". A small, free site for finding visual novels by describing what you want in your own words.

Questions

Is this site free?

Yes, completely. There's no paid version, no account, no ads and no tracking. The source code is open on GitHub under the MIT license, so anyone can read it, change it, or share it.

What does it do, exactly?

You type something like "fantasy setting, female lead, multiple endings". It works out which VNDB tags, languages, engines, platforms, lengths and dates you mean, shows them as labels so you can check them, then asks VNDB for matching titles. Click a cover for its details: synopsis, rating, length, staff, tags, screenshots and related titles.

Where does the information come from?

From VNDB's official public API, live, each time you search. Your browser asks VNDB directly; there's no server of ours in between. It doesn't scrape websites.

Is this made by VNDB?

No. It's an independent fan project that uses VNDB's free API. If you like it, the people to thank are VNDB's maintainers and the volunteers who fill in its database.

Can I download or play games through it?

No. It's a search tool only. It shows information and links to the title's VNDB page and official sites. To play something, buy it or get it from its official source.

Are the covers and screenshots stored anywhere?

No. Your browser loads them straight from VNDB's image server, the same way vndb.org does. Nothing is copied or stored by this site.

A title is missing, or its information is wrong.

Everything shown comes from VNDB, so corrections belong there. Anyone can edit VNDB, and fixes show up here too. Also check that it isn't hidden by your Always exclude or Not interested lists, and remember that titles with sexual content aren't shown here.

Why is it all ages only?

Titles with sexual content are never shown on this site, and suggestive or violent covers are blurred until clicked. Showing adult content publicly would require real age checks under laws like the UK Online Safety Act. The desktop version, which runs on your own computer, has an optional Mature switch for adults. It's free, and the install steps are on that page.

Does anything about me get sent anywhere?

Only your searches, which go straight from your browser to VNDB to get results. Your preferences and hidden titles are saved in your own browser (local storage), never on a server. There are no cookies, ads or analytics.

I own something shown here and want it removed.

Covers, screenshots and descriptions are served by VNDB, so please contact them at contact@vndb.org. Once it's gone from VNDB, it disappears here too. For anything about this site itself, open an issue on GitHub.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

Open an issue on GitHub. Including the exact search you typed helps a lot.

About Yoru no Toshokan

夜の図書館, "the night library". A small, free program for finding visual novels by describing what you want in your own words.

Questions

Is this program free?

Yes, completely. There's no paid version, no account, no ads and no tracking. The source code is open on GitHub under the MIT license, so anyone can read it, change it, or share it.

What does it do, exactly?

You type something like "fantasy setting, female lead, multiple endings". It works out which VNDB tags, languages, engines, platforms, lengths and dates you mean, shows them as labels so you can check them, then asks VNDB for matching titles. Click a cover for its details: synopsis, rating, length, staff, tags, screenshots and related titles.

Where does the information come from?

From VNDB's official public API, live, each time you search. Answers are kept on your computer for a day so repeated searches don't bother VNDB's servers. It doesn't scrape websites.

Is this made by VNDB?

No. It's an independent fan project that uses VNDB's free API. If you like it, the people to thank are VNDB's maintainers and the volunteers who fill in its database.

Can I download or play games through it?

No. It's a search tool only. It shows information and links to the title's VNDB page and official sites. To play something, buy it or get it from its official source.

Are the covers and screenshots stored anywhere?

No. Your browser loads them straight from VNDB's image server, the same way vndb.org does. Nothing is copied or saved by this program.

A title is missing, or its information is wrong.

Everything shown comes from VNDB, so corrections belong there. Anyone can edit VNDB, and fixes show up here too. Also check that it isn't hidden by the Mature content switch, your Always exclude list, or your Not interested list.

What does the Mature content switch do?

Off (the default): nothing with sexual content is shown, and suggestive or violent covers are blurred until clicked. On: only titles with sexual content are shown. It's for adults only.

Does anything about me get sent anywhere?

Only your searches, which go to VNDB to get results. Your preferences, hidden titles and settings stay in a file on your own computer.

What is "LLM rewrite"?

An optional helper for long, chatty sentences. If you've installed Ollama, a model running on your own computer turns your sentence into short keywords before searching. It's off by default, and nothing is sent online.

I own something shown here and want it removed.

Covers, screenshots and descriptions are served by VNDB, so please contact them at contact@vndb.org. Once it's gone from VNDB, it disappears here too, within a day. For anything about this program itself, open an issue on GitHub.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

Open an issue on GitHub. Including the exact search you typed helps a lot.
